From Numbers to Code: My First Steps in Web Development

Transitioning from a commerce background into the world of web development has been both a challenging and rewarding experience. While the two fields may appear unrelated at first glance, I have come to realize that they share a common foundation — logical reasoning, attention to detail, and structured problem-solving.

In this post, I reflect on my initial steps into coding, beginning with the core building blocks of the web: HTML and CSS. HTML provides the structural framework of a webpage, while CSS brings it to life through design and visual presentation. Together, they form the foundation upon which nearly every website we interact with today is built.

This journey has reinforced an important lesson: skills from one discipline often translate meaningfully into another, and a willingness to learn is the true starting point of growth.
